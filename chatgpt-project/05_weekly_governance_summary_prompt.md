# Weekly Governance Summary Prompt

Review the PMO operations log for the cycle and produce a weekly governance summary.

Output sections:
1. Executive summary
2. Decisions needed
3. Escalation candidates
4. Overdue or blocked actions
5. Stakeholder follow-ups
6. Risks, issues, and dependencies
7. Weak or stale status signals
8. Value follow-up actions and measurement gaps
9. Project plan update recommendations
10. Items to carry into the next governance meeting
11. Clarification questions

Keep the summary decision-oriented and operational.

For value follow-up, separate missing baselines, missing actuals, finance
validation, stale sources, owner gaps, proxy-only metrics, and high realization
risk. Include owner, due date, escalation path, and next evidence source when
known.
